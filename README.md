Landing a Software Engineering internship is a numbers game. With hundreds of applications per open role, your resume has approximately 6 seconds to pass both an ATS scanner and a recruiter's eye before it gets archived.

I built this template specifically for SWE interns and new grads who have limited work experience but strong projects and academic achievements. The section ordering is deliberate: it puts your technical skills and projects at the top (where you are strongest), and de-emphasizes experience gaps (where you are weakest).

This is the exact format I used to land my own internship offers, and it has been used by 3,500+ engineers across 18+ countries.

### ⚡ The Internship Resume Strategy: How to Win With Limited Experience

The biggest mistake interns make is building a resume that looks like a senior engineer's — trying to pad it with jargon. Recruiters at top companies review thousands of intern resumes. They are specifically looking for:

1. **GPA / Academic Excellence** (if it is strong, it goes at the top)
2. **2-3 High-Quality Projects** with quantified impact (users, performance, scale)
3. **Competitive Programming** (LeetCode, Codeforces rating as social proof)
4. **Relevant Technical Skills** that match the exact JD keywords

This template's structure is pre-optimized for exactly this order.
